Frequently Asked Questions

Search above or browse by topic

How does billing work for ongoing projects?
+

Fixed-scope projects are billed in milestones — typically 30% upfront, 40% at midpoint, and 30% on delivery. Ongoing retainer work is billed monthly in advance.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?
+

Milestone payments are refundable for undelivered work. Once a milestone is delivered and approved, that portion becomes non-refundable — this is covered in your project agreement.

What happens if my site goes down after launch?
+

All launched projects include 30 days of post-launch monitoring at no cost. After that, uptime monitoring and incident response are available under a support retainer.

Do you provide the source code and hosting access?
+

Yes. You get full repository access and hosting/infrastructure credentials on delivery — nothing is held back or licensed exclusively to us.

How do I update the email on my account?
+

Go to Settings → Account in your dashboard and update your email. You'll get a confirmation link at the new address before the change takes effect.

Can multiple team members access one account?
+

Yes, you can invite teammates with view or admin roles from Settings → Team. There's no limit on the number of seats.

How long does a typical project take?
+

Most web projects take 4–8 weeks from kickoff to launch. Mobile apps and larger platform builds typically run 10–16 weeks depending on scope.

Can I request changes after a project is delivered?
+

Small fixes within 30 days of launch are included. Beyond that, changes are scoped and quoted as a new engagement or covered under a support retainer.
